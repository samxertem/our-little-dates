# Privacy Policy — our little dates

**Effective date:** 15 November 2026

"our little dates" (the **app**) is a small, personal app for keeping a board of date ideas,
memories and photos with your partner. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information.

---

## In short

- **Everything stays on your device** unless you turn on sharing.
- **Sharing is optional.** If you share a board with your partner, your board is stored online
  so your devices can sync. Only devices that joined with your board's invite code can see it.
- **No accounts, no email, no phone number, no passwords.**
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking, nothing is sold** or given to anyone for marketing.

---

## 1. Information kept on your device

When you use the app, it saves what you create in the app's private storage on your device:

- your date ideas: titles, plans/notes, tags, cost, card look, stickers, "done" dates and memories
- the two names you enter (for example, your name and your partner's name)
- photos you add to a date (the app resizes them and saves a copy as a JPEG)
- small settings, such as the season theme and how many times you've petted Mochi the cat

This information does not leave your device unless you turn on sharing (section 2).
Uninstalling the app deletes it from the device.

## 2. Optional: the shared board

In **♡ us → our shared board** you can start sharing, or join your partner's board with an
invite code (like `MOCHI-XXXX-XXXX`). Only if you do this:

**What is stored online:**
- the information listed in section 1 for that board: date ideas and their notes/memories,
  the two names, the season setting, Mochi's pet count, and the photos added to dates
- a random, anonymous identifier for each device that joined the board (created automatically;
  it is not linked to your name, email or phone number)

**Who can see it:**
- only the devices that joined the board with its invite code. This is enforced by the database's
  own access rules, not just by the app.
- the app's developer, as the administrator of the database, can technically access stored data.
  The developer does not look at your board's contents, except to help you when you ask
  (for example, to delete a board).

**Where and how it's stored:**
- with [Supabase](https://supabase.com), a database and file-storage provider, in its
  **European Union region (Frankfurt, Germany)**. Supabase processes this data only to provide
  the service to the app.
- all data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Supabase also encrypts stored data at rest.
- like most online services, Supabase may briefly process technical information such as your IP
  address to deliver the service and protect it against abuse (for example, rate limiting).

**Photos:** to make sure a shared memory is never lost by accident, photos uploaded to a shared
board are not removed when a photo is taken off a date. They are deleted together with the
board (see section 4).

## 3. What the app never does

- no advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools
- no selling, renting or sharing of your information with third parties
  (Supabase only stores it on the app's behalf, as described above)
- no access to your contacts, location, microphone, or other apps' data

**Permissions:** the app uses the internet (only for the optional shared board). When you add a
photo, your device's own photo picker opens and the app only receives the photos you choose.
On iPhone/iPad the app may ask for camera access if you choose to take a new photo.

## 4. Keeping and deleting your information

- **On your device:** delete any date in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
- **"Stop sharing on this device"** (in ♡ us) disconnects that device from the board. The
  information already on the device stays there.
- **Deleting a date while sharing** removes its content from the shared board as well.
- **Deleting a whole shared board** (all its dates, photos and the anonymous device identifiers):
  contact the developer (section 6) from either partner's side, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Shared boards are kept for as long as you use them, or until you ask for them to be deleted.

## 5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## 6. Contact and your rights

You can ask for a copy of your shared board's data, or for it to be corrected or deleted,
at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the KVKK in
Türkiye), you may have further rights, which we will respect.

**Contact:** the developer email shown on the app's Google Play Store page, or open an issue at
[github.com/samxertem/our-little-dates](https://github.com/samxertem/our-little-dates/issues).

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
